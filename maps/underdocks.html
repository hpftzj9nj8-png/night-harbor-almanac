How to move
  • Drag to turn, right-drag (two fingers) to slide.
  • Scroll or pinch zooms toward the pointer.
  • Double-click a spot to fly there.
  • Walk lets you go down streets and corridors.

Built from the game's own scene files (game build 0.30.2.1). Pins: signs, teleport pads, camps, harvest nodes and other things placed in those files. NPCs and named mobs aren't in the files; they get pinned as you play. Drag "Slice off the top" to see inside buildings and dungeons.
